DUPLEX SCANNING WITHOUT A DUPLEX SCANNER

Duplexn't: no duplex, both sides anyway!

If your scanner’s document feeder only does one side, there’s no need to scan both sides of every page by hand. Seriously, why would you? It’s the 21st century, AI is going to wipe us all out in a couple of years, and you’re wasting time on manual labor? Just install Duplexn't already!

SEVERAL DOCUMENTS

Splitting into documents

An invoice, a bill from the dentist and some random scrap of paper from the insurance company… Scan them all together, then mark where one ends and the next begins — and save them all in one go.

STRAIGHTENING

Skew correction

If a page comes out crooked, Duplexn’t will straighten it automatically. What? Yes! Pure magic! (Not really — just a sweatshop full of tiny straightening elves.)

And there’s more…

01

Reordering

Drag any page back and forth. Or forth and back. Or here and there. Top to bottom, bottom to top. Amazing!

02

Emptiness detector

There’s plenty of emptiness in us already. Some people are made entirely of it. Let’s at least get the blank backs out of our documents.

03

Missing pages

Lazy scanner skipped a page? To hell with it: the app will tell you a page is missing and let you add it to the batch.

04

Full Ctrl+Z

Undo any change at any moment, all the way back to the original. Too bad life doesn’t work like that. Or does it? No, it doesn’t.

05

180° rotation

Loaded the backs upside down? Ha-ha! Well, not everyone is born a genius. Duplexn't can turn them all right side up at once. Ta-da!

WHAT IS THIS

What’s all this for?

I made this app because I got tired of scanning the paperwork that bureaucratic reality so generously showers me with, and because the software from printer makers, and every other program that can do something similar, is TERRIBLE.

Duplexn't should work with any device that supports eSCL, but, for obvious reasons, it has only been tested with my home Epson ET-3850 (and it’s tired already). So for the rest of the world this is a beta. Or even an alpha. Put simply, Duplexn't may work for you, sort of. Or not at all. That’s fixable, but it takes

MOTIVATION

…and requests in Issues. Nothing motivates me to crack the whip over the code-straightening elves like fresh issues. But keep in mind: if it’s about supporting other devices, you’ll have to help with testing. I’m not about to start a home collection of MFPs.
